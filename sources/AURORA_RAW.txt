About
What this bounty is for
Integrate into any Monad app you're building (swap, deposit, or custom execution) using Aurora Intents, powered by NEAR Intents (Swap API, Intents Deposits, Intents Connect).
What Aurora Intents brings to a Monad app:
• A persistent, any-chain deposit address — one address per user that accepts funds from 31+ chains, non-EVM included, with no separate deposit flow to build per chain
• Built-in cross-chain swap — users fund or interact with the app using whatever asset they hold; Aurora routes and swaps into exactly what the app needs
• Deposit & execute in one flow — using Intents Connect, a deposit can trigger a smart contract call directly: stake, lend, mint, or open a position the moment funds land
• A bigger addressable user base — anyone holding assets on supported chains can use the app immediately
• Custom fees — apps can set their own fee on every cross-chain flow, with a 40–60% revenue share in favor of the integrator

Judging criteria
What judges look for
Ships a working integration of at least one Aurora Intents product (Intents Connect, Swap API, or Intents Deposits), demoed live — an actual working flow, not mocked

Removes a real cross-chain friction point for the end user — solves a credible user problem

Technical quality: correct settlement/refund handling, clean UX with cross-chain complexity hidden from the user, an easy-to-understand flow that avoids unnecessary actions

Fit and creativity within its chosen track (trading, consumer/payments, social, or infra)

Bonus: multi-chain source coverage, contract-level composability via Intents Connect, or a Confidential Intents use case

Deliverables
What to have ready
A working integration of at least one Aurora Intents product (Intents Connect, Swap API, or Intents Deposits), demoed live — not mocked

A live, working cross-chain flow showing funds arriving from another chain and being used within the Monad app

Standard Metropolis submission requirements also apply — see track-level deliverables (public repo, technical demo video, pitch video, live product link)
